插空法示範Slot-in method demo

4 名男生與 3 名女生排成一列，女生互不相鄰 → 4! × P(5,3) = 1440。按「下一步」逐步看。 Four boys and three girls in a row with no two girls adjacent → 4! × P(5,3) = 1440. Press Next to walk through it.

男生可以點兩下互換位置；第 4 步點空隙就可以放入女生（再點最後一位可以拎返）。 Tap two boys to swap them; on step 4 tap a gap to place a girl (tap the last one again to take her back).

兩類重疊示範Overlap demo

20 人會排球、25 人會籃球，其中 15 人兩樣都會 → 全隊 20 + 25 − 15 = 30 人。 20 players can play volleyball and 25 can play basketball, with 15 doing both → 20 + 25 − 15 = 30 players.

按「−／＋」改變重疊人數，睇下總數點變；再想想「至少一個」為何常用反面計數。 Use −／+ to change the overlap and watch the total; then think about why "at least one" is often counted by complement.

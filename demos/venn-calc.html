文氏圖計算器Venn-diagram calculator

六個數量：A、B、A and B、A or B、Total、not A nor B。輸入其中幾個，其他會自動計出（自動格會鎖住）。圖中的圓固定不動；兩者無交集時交集區顯示 0。 Six quantities: A, B, A and B, A or B, Total, not A nor B. Enter some of them and the rest are worked out (those cells lock). The circles never move; if there is no overlap the middle region shows 0.

試試：A = 20、B = 15、A and B = 5 → A or B 自動變成 30；再輸入 Total = 50 → not A nor B 自動變成 20。 Try: A = 20, B = 15, A and B = 5 → A or B becomes 30; then enter Total = 50 → not A nor B becomes 20.

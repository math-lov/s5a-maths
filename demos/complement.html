至少／至多示範At-least / at-most demo

6 男 7 女中選 5 人，至少 1 男 1 女：直接分類 4 個 case，反面只需 2 個。 Choose 5 of 6 boys and 7 girls with at least one of each: 4 cases directly, only 2 for the complement.

第 2、3 步可以點任何一行 case，看它揀了哪幾個人（其餘的點會淡出）。 On steps 2 and 3, tap a case row to see which people it picks (the rest fade out).

分組問題示範Grouping demo

組別有無編號，答案差一個階乘：6 人分 2 組 = 20 或 10；10 人分 4、4、2 = 3150 或 1575。 Labels change the answer by a factorial: 6 people into 2 groups = 20 or 10; 10 people into 4, 4, 2 = 3150 or 1575.

切換上面兩個情境，再用「隨機再分一次」看看不同分法；無組名時按「示範對調」會見到重複計數的原因。 Switch scenes and press Shuffle for other divisions; with unlabelled groups, press Swap to see why the counting repeats.

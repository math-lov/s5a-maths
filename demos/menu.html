午餐套裝示範Lunch-set demo

餐牌：前菜 2 款、主菜 4 款、飲品 3 款。三樣都要揀（分步）就是 2 × 4 × 3 = 24；若只買一樣（分類）就是 2 + 4 + 3 = 9。 Menu: 2 appetizers, 4 main dishes, 3 drinks. Choosing all three (steps) gives 2 × 4 × 3 = 24 sets; buying only one item (cases) gives 2 + 4 + 3 = 9.

口訣：見到「或者」＝分類用加；見到「同時／然後」＝分步用乘。 Rule of thumb: "or" means cases (add); "and then" means steps (multiply).

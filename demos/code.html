密碼逐位示範Digit-by-digit demo

按數字填入 4 個位，下面顯示「每一位有幾個選擇」：可重複是 10 × 10 × 10 × 10；不可重複是 10 × 9 × 8 × 7；首位不可為 0 就先處理首位。 Tap digits to fill the four places and watch "choices left at this digit": repetition gives 10^4, no repetition gives 10 × 9 × 8 × 7, and a restricted first digit is handled first.

「用過唔可以再用」＝每步少一個選擇；「首位不可以是 0」＝先把有規限的位置數清楚。 "A digit used cannot be used again" = one fewer choice each step; "the first digit cannot be 0" = count the restricted place first.

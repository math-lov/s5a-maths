用法How to use

1. 打開一份測驗 → 左上是題號（A1…A5、B1…Bonus），可按題號直接跳去任何一題。 Open a quiz → the top strip shows question numbers (A1…A5, B1…Bonus); tap one to jump straight to it.

2. 每題一頁：題目與逐步題解同頁展示，選擇題可以按選項即時對答案。 One question per page: the question and the worked solution are shown together; click an option to check instantly.

3. 想先自己做完才看題解？按右上角「收起題解」，做完再按「顯示題解」。 Want to finish the paper first? Press 'Hide solutions' at the top, then 'Show solutions' when done.

4. 右上角的「中文／EN／中英」一鍵切換全站語言（題目、選項與題解都有中英兩版）。 The '中文／EN／中英' switch at the top right changes the language of the whole site (questions, options and solutions all have both versions).

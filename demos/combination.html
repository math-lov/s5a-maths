組合示範Combination demo

5 人選 3 人組隊：有次序 P(5,3) = 60，唔計次序 C(5,3) = 60 ÷ 3! = 10。 Choose 3 of 5: with order P(5,3) = 60; ignoring order C(5,3) = 60 ÷ 3! = 10.

點上面 3 位學生會移落隊伍列（點隊伍成員可以放返）；「打亂次序」會換次序後自動排返，示範「次序唔算新一隊」。 Tap 3 students to move them into the team row (tap a member to send them back); Shuffle shows another order, then snaps back.

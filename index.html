這裡沒有分數，也沒有排名。 答錯只代表「還未學到」，不代表你不行；「已掌握」的記錄只存在你自己的瀏覽器裡，隨時可以重新開始。 No scores here, and no ranking. Getting one wrong only means "not yet" — not that you can't. Your "mastered" marks stay in your own browser and you can start over any time.

本站的讀法是「先試，後明白（try first, understand later）」：先在紙上自己做一次；卡住了才按提示鍵要一個小提示；做完才看完整題解。直接看答案，是最快忘記的方法。 How this site works: "try first, understand later" — work every question out on paper yourself; ask the hint key for one small nudge only when you are stuck; read the full explanation after you have finished. Reading the answer straight away is the fastest way to forget it.
測驗檢討Quiz reviews
用法How to use
1
選一份檢討：按下面「第 10 章 測驗檢討」進入。進入後最上面那條是題號（A1…A5、B1…Bonus），按任何一個可以直接跳去那一題；每題一頁。 Open a review: tap "Chapter 10 Quiz Review" below. The top strip lists the question numbers (A1…A5, B1…Bonus) — tap one to jump straight to it. One question per page.
2
開工前先按右上角「收起題解」：整頁只會剩題目，跟考試一樣，答案預先看不到。（預設是顯示題解，所以每次開始前都要先按這一下。） Before you start, press "Hide solutions" at the top right: only the question stays on screen, like in the exam, with nothing spoiling it. (Solutions are shown by default, so press this first every session.)
3
先自己做一次：
· 選擇題：點選項 → 按「檢查答案」；第一次錯只會顯示「再試一次」，同一題第二次錯才把正確答案標成綠色。
· 長題目：先在紙上寫你自己的步驟，才去對答案。 Try it yourself first:
· Multiple choice: pick an option, then press "Check answer". One wrong attempt just says "Try again"; a second wrong attempt marks the correct answer in green.
· Long questions: write your own working on paper first, then compare with the answer.
4
卡住了？用提示鍵（hint key）：在「已收起題解」的狀態下會見到「顯示提示（第 n / N 步）」；每按一次只揭曉下一步，是「給方向」而不是直接給答案。揭到最後一步會出現「顯示全部題解」。用過幾多提示，網站會按題目記住。 Stuck? Use the hint key: while solutions are hidden you will see "Show hint (step n / N)". Each press reveals only the next step — a nudge rather than the whole solution. Once you reach the last hint, "Show all steps" appears. How many hints you have opened is remembered per question.
5
做完（或提示用完）才看題解：按右上角「顯示題解」或下面的「顯示全部題解」，一次過看到全部步驟（連 (1M) 方法分、(1A) 答案分）、圖、「為甚麼會選錯」的陷阱分析，以及「帶得走的技巧」。 Read the explanation after you finish: press "Show solutions" at the top right, or "Show all steps" below it, to see every step at once (with (1M) method and (1A) accuracy marks), the figures, "why the other options are wrong", and the take-away tip.
6
覺得掌握了？按題目下方的「標記為已掌握」，該題號會變成綠色 ✓。記錄只存在你自己的瀏覽器（既不上載，也不計分）：再按一次可取消，主目錄底部有「清除本機『已掌握』記錄」。 Got it? Press "Mark as mastered" below the question and that question number turns green ✓. The record lives in your own browser only (nothing is uploaded and nothing is scored): press again to undo, or use "Clear my 'mastered' records" at the bottom of this page.
7
仍有唔明白？交給 AI 追問：按題目下方的「複製提問 Prompt（整題）」，或每個步驟旁邊的「問 AI」—— 網站會自動幫你寫好題目、你的疑問和該步驟，貼去任何 AI 都可以繼續追問。 Still unsure? Hand it to an AI: press "Copy prompt (whole question)" under the question, or "Ask AI" next to any step — the site writes the question, your doubt and that step into a prompt; paste it into any AI and keep asking until it clicks.
8
語言：右上角「中文／EN／中英」一鍵切換。題目與選項固定用英文（與考試卷一致），詳解則中英並列。

## Verified editorial corrections

* Q3: the official solution writes OD perpendicular AB in one line, although D
  does not exist in this figure. The correct segment is OM.
* Q19: the official solution writes CBE + BCD. The four vertices here are
  B,C,D,E; the correct opposite-angle equation is CBE + CDE = 180°.
* Q21: the last triangle in the official solution is labelled ADE. A,D,E are
  collinear. The equation belongs to triangle ABD.
* Q24: the official solution prints r²=169 immediately before r=39. The correct
  sum is 36²+225=1521. The official final answer 39 cm is correct.
* Q33(a): the official solution's last line names ABCD, already a given circle.
  The conclusion established by that argument is ABEF cyclic.

Each affected question identifies the correction in its bilingual tip. No
missing condition is silently added. Full DOCX plain-text extraction loses arc
hats and some formula symbols: Q13, Q27–Q30, MC5 and MC8 were checked directly
against the PDF diagrams, not interpreted as straight-chord lengths or ratios.

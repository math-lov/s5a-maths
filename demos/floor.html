下捨入示範Rounding-down demo

由 1 到 n 之中，x 的倍數數目＝⌊n ÷ x⌋：先除，再向下取最接近的整數。62 ÷ 3 = 20.66… 只得 20 個，因為第 21 個是 63，已經超出 62；若 n 剛好是 x 的倍數（60 ÷ 3 = 20）就直接除，唔使捨。 The number of multiples of x from 1 to n is ⌊n ÷ x⌋: divide, then take the nearest integer below. 62 ÷ 3 = 20.66… gives 20, because the 21st multiple is 63, already past 62; when n is an exact multiple of x (60 ÷ 3 = 20) the division is already whole and nothing is discarded.

口訣：除完之後，小數部分一律捨去（唔可以四捨五入）。 Rule of thumb: division first, then always drop the decimal part — never round to the nearest.
